Data and methods
Where the numbers come from.

Two public datasets from the New York State Department of Health, one cleaning pipeline, and a short list of rules applied everywhere.

Sources

Dataset What it is Used for
Hospital Inpatient Discharges (SPARCS De-Identified): 2023 One row per inpatient discharge in New York State in 2023: 2,125,754 rows, 33 columns Charges, costs, condition, severity, age group, length of stay, payer
Health Facility General Information One row per licensed facility Ownership type and coordinates for the map

Both are downloaded through the Health Data NY API. No raw download is stored in the repository.

The five conditions

Condition APR-DRG Discharges, 2023 Hospitals Hospitals with 30+ stays
Heart failure 194 59,668 189 159
Major respiratory infections (mostly COVID-19) 137 35,044 190 156
Ischemic stroke 045 26,529 182 126
Sepsis 720 131,139 187 164
Kidney and urinary tract infections 463 30,248 188 148

One acute medical condition per organ system, all among the ten most common groups in 2023. Childbirth, newborn and psychiatric groups were left out because their care settings are different.

Cleaning

Placeholder list, to be checked against data_pipeline.Rmd:

  • Stays with no hospital identifier are dropped (184 of 282,628), leaving 282,444.
  • Charges and costs arrive as text with commas and are converted to numbers.
  • Length of stay recorded as “120 +” is set to 120 and flagged.
  • Severity, age group and payer are stored as ordered categories.
  • Hospital identifiers are matched to the facility file for ownership and location; 186 of 192 hospitals match.

Rules used in every analysis

  1. Stays with estimated costs below $500 are excluded.
  2. Rankings, comparisons across conditions and regression use hospitals with at least 30 stays for the condition. The funnel plot uses every hospital.
  3. Stays where charges are below costs are kept.

Variables

Variable Meaning
charges Total amount billed for the stay (primary outcome)
costs The state’s estimate of what the stay cost the hospital (secondary outcome)
condition One of the five conditions
severity Minor, Moderate, Major, Extreme
age_group Five age bands
los Length of stay in days
payer Medicare, Medicaid, Private, Other
ownership Nonprofit or Public

Methods in brief

Placeholder paragraph: bootstrap intervals for hospital medians; regression on the log scale fitted separately for each condition; cross-validation to compare models; rank correlation across conditions.

Limitations

  • Charges and estimated costs do not show what patients or insurers actually paid.
  • The data are observational; we describe differences and do not claim causes.
  • Sepsis coding intensity differs across hospitals.
  • The respiratory group in 2023 is mostly COVID-19.
