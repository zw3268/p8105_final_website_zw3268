Question 1
How far apart are hospitals?

For each of the five conditions, we compare the median charge and the median estimated cost at every hospital with at least 30 stays.

The size of the gap

# Demo numbers. The real table is produced by the Q1 analysis.
hospital_medians |>
  filter(n_stays >= min_cases) |>
  group_by(Condition = condition) |>
  summarize(
    Hospitals = n(),
    `Charges: P90 / P10` = quantile(median_charges, 0.9) / quantile(median_charges, 0.1),
    `Costs: P90 / P10` = quantile(median_costs, 0.9) / quantile(median_costs, 0.1)
  ) |>
  knitr::kable(digits = 1)

Hospital rankings

This is what a finished figure looks like with the site’s shared theme (theme_site() in R/site_helpers.R).

hospital_medians |>
  filter(condition == "Heart failure", n_stays >= min_cases) |>
  mutate(
    facility_name = fct_reorder(facility_name, median_charges),
    ratio = median_charges / median(median_charges)
  ) |>
  ggplot(aes(x = median_charges, y = facility_name)) +
  geom_linerange(aes(xmin = charges_low, xmax = charges_high), color = site_rule) +
  geom_point(aes(color = log2(ratio)), size = 1.4) +
  scale_x_log10(labels = scales::label_dollar()) +
  scale_color_gradient2(low = site_low, mid = site_mid, high = site_high, guide = "none") +
  labs(
    x = "Median charge per stay (log scale)",
    y = "Hospitals",
    caption = "Simulated data. Bars are 95% bootstrap intervals."
  ) +
  theme_site() +
  theme(axis.text.y = element_blank(), panel.grid.major.y = element_blank())

What to keep in mind

  • Charges are the amounts billed, not what patients or insurers paid.
  • Costs are estimates produced by the state.
  • Hospitals with fewer than 30 stays for a condition are left out of rankings but appear in the funnel plot.
  • “The typical hospital” means the median of the hospital medians for a condition, not the median of all stays.
